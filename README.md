PoultryFit Kenya Project Progress Story

Over the past few weeks, our PoultryFit Kenya project has continued to make remarkable progress through teamwork and collaboration among all members. From the beginning, every department was assigned specific responsibilities to ensure that the final product would provide poultry farmers with practical and reliable solutions.
The software development team began by setting up the project's GitHub repositories and organizing the workflow that would allow everyone to work together efficiently. They created separate repositories for the frontend, backend, and machine learning sections of the project, making it easier for every team member to contribute while maintaining quality and proper version control.
The frontend developers then focused on designing the application's user interface. They developed the main screens that farmers will interact with, including poultry planning, disease diagnosis, feed planning, and veterinary services. Their goal has been to ensure that the application is simple to use while displaying real information collected from the research team instead of sample data.
At the same time, the backend team concentrated on building the system that powers the application. They designed the database to store poultry bylaws, agrovet prices, disease information, and user records. They also developed the application logic that connects the frontend to the machine learning model so that whenever a farmer submits information, the system processes it and returns accurate recommendations.
The machine learning team has also made significant progress by developing a poultry disease classification model. They have been training the model using poultry disease datasets so that it can identify common poultry diseases from symptoms and images. Their work is aimed at helping farmers receive quick and accurate disease diagnosis, making poultry management more efficient.
On the research side, extensive work has been carried out to ensure that the application is based on real and verified information. Research has involved collecting county and ward-level poultry bylaws, translating them into simple language that farmers can easily understand, and gathering agrovet prices from local suppliers around Juja. This information is being organized into structured spreadsheets before being integrated into the backend database, ensuring that all recommendations provided by the application are supported by actual field data.
One of the most memorable moments of the project came during Week Three, when the team visited the poultry farm at JKUAT JHUB Africa. The visit provided an opportunity to interact directly with a practicing poultry farmer and gain practical knowledge beyond classroom learning. During the visit, the farmer in charge welcomed us and explained the day-to-day management of a poultry farm. He demonstrated proper poultry housing, feeding practices, vaccination schedules, disease prevention methods, biosecurity measures, record keeping, and the challenges that poultry farmers commonly experience. He also shared valuable insights on poultry production costs, marketing opportunities, and the importance of maintaining healthy birds through proper management. The team actively asked questions throughout the session, making the visit both educational and inspiring. The knowledge gained during this visit will greatly improve the quality of information that will be incorporated into the PoultryFit system.
Throughout the project, teamwork has remained our greatest strength. Regular meetings have enabled members from different departments to share updates, solve challenges together, and ensure that every component fits perfectly with the others. The GitHub collaboration workflow has made it possible for developers, researchers, and other team members to contribute effectively while keeping the project organized.
As we continue with the project, our focus is now on completing the remaining features, integrating all components into one functional application, expanding our research database, improving the machine learning model, and carrying out thorough system testing before the final presentation.
The PoultryFit Kenya project has grown steadily from an idea into a practical solution that has the potential to transform poultry farming by providing farmers with accurate, reliable, and locally relevant information. Every team member has played an important role, and with the progress made so far, we are confident that the final system will successfully meet the needs of poultry farmers across Kenya
