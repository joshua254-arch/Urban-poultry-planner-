1. Executive Summary
Urban PoultryFit is a digital project developed to support urban poultry farmers by providing useful information and tools that can help them make better poultry-farming decisions. The project focused on understanding the challenges faced by poultry farmers, including poultry housing, feeding, vaccination, common poultry diseases, access to agrovet information, poultry regulations and management practices. The team conducted field visits and interacted with poultry farmers to understand real farming practices and identify areas where technology could provide assistance. The project combines frontend development, backend development and machine-learning components, supported by research and data collected from poultry farms, poultry bylaws and agrovet prices. The overall goal was to make poultry farming information more accessible and help urban farmers improve productivity, management and decision-making.

4. Challenges Faced
During the development of Urban PoultryFit, the team experienced several challenges, summarized below alongside how each was addressed.
5. Lessons Learned
5.1 Field Research Is Important
We learned that it is important to interact with actual users before developing a technological solution. The farm visits helped us understand the actual needs and challenges of poultry farmers.
5.2 Teamwork Is Essential
The project demonstrated that a successful technological solution requires people with different skills. Frontend development, backend development, machine learning and field research all contributed to the final project.
5.3 Data Quality Matters
We learned that the usefulness of a digital system depends greatly on the quality of the information and data used. Data collected from farmers, agrovet outlets and other sources must be accurate and properly organized.
5.4 Technology Can Support Agriculture
The project showed us that technology can be applied to agriculture to improve access to information and support farmers in making decisions.
5.5 User Needs Should Come First
A system should not only focus on technical features. It should solve an actual problem faced by its intended users. Understanding farmers' needs therefore played an important role in developing Urban PoultryFit.
5.6 What We Would Do Differently
In a future version, we would begin collecting and organizing the required datasets earlier, involve more poultry farmers during the testing stage and allow more time for testing the different components together before final deployment.
6. Recommendations
6.1 Regularly Update Agrovet Prices
Agrovet prices should be updated regularly because prices of poultry inputs and products can change over time.
6.2 Expand the Poultry Database
The system should continue building a larger database containing information about poultry breeds, diseases, feeds, vaccination schedules, management practices and other relevant poultry information.
6.3 Improve Machine-Learning Accuracy
As more reliable data becomes available, the machine-learning component should be trained and tested using larger and better-quality datasets to improve its performance.
6.4 Involve More Farmers
Future development should involve more urban poultry farmers during testing. Their feedback can help developers identify areas that need improvement.
6.5 Continue Research on Poultry Regulations
The poultry bylaws section should be reviewed and updated whenever relevant regulations or requirements change.
6.6 Improve the System Continuously
Urban PoultryFit should be treated as a system that can continue developing after the initial project. Future teams can add new features based on farmers' needs and technological developments.

8. Conclusion
The Urban PoultryFit project demonstrated how technology, agricultural research and teamwork can be combined to address challenges faced by urban poultry farmers. Through field visits, the team gained practical knowledge about poultry housing, feeding, vaccination, diseases, hygiene and general farm management. Research into poultry bylaws and agrovet prices further contributed to the project's information base. At the technical level, the project brought together frontend development, backend development and machine learning to create a digital solution for poultry farming. Although the team faced challenges involving data collection, technical development, coordination and understanding practical farming conditions, these challenges provided valuable learning experiences. Urban PoultryFit provides a foundation that can be improved through additional data, farmer feedback, system testing and future technological development.
